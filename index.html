Good to know

A few frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before your pet's first visit.

How long does a grooming appointment take?

Timing depends on your pet's size, coat, temperament and selected service. We'll discuss an estimated timeframe when confirming your appointment.

What should I bring to the appointment?

Bring your pet on a secure leash or in an appropriate carrier. Please share any relevant grooming preferences, sensitivities or special care instructions.

Can I request a particular haircut?

Absolutely. You can describe the look you want or bring a reference photo. We'll discuss what is suitable for your pet's coat and comfort.

Do you groom puppies?

Yes, puppy introductions can help young pets become more comfortable with grooming. The appropriate service depends on their age and individual needs.

Are the prices fixed?

The prices shown are starting prices for this website demonstration. Actual grooming prices may vary based on size, coat condition, temperament and service requirements.
